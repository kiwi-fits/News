# 🎥 24/7 AI Tamil News Reels Publisher

Fully automated 24/7 AI-powered 9:16 vertical video reel generator and Facebook publisher for Tamil Breaking News and Evergreen stories (History, Science, General Knowledge, Wisdom Stories).

---

### 🌟 Key Capabilities
- ⚡ **Real-Time News Monitoring**: Checks Sri Lankan Tamil news feeds (Virakesari, Thinakkural, etc.) continuously.
- 🎙️ **Neural Voiceover**: Powered by Microsoft Edge-TTS with **Valluvar** (Tamil male news anchor voice) at **+12% energetic pacing**.
- 🗣️ **English-to-Tamil Transliteration**: Automatically transliterates English terms and acronyms (`7wonders` ➔ `7 ஒண்டெர்ஸ்`, `CCTV` ➔ `சிசிடிவி`, `AI` ➔ `ஏஐ`) so the voice never stumbles.
- 🎞️ **9:16 Vertical HD Reels**: Composed with FFmpeg, multi-image Ken Burns cinematic camera pans, brand logo canvas, and ambient news background music.
- 🏛️ **Evergreen Stories Fallback**: If news is quiet for 30 minutes, automatically generates captivating reels covering History, Science wonders, Did You Know?, or Wisdom stories.
- 🚀 **Auto-Publish to Facebook**: Automatically posts directly to your Facebook Page with captions, hashtags, and engagement questions.
- ☁️ **24/7 Cloud Automation via GitHub Actions**: Runs continuously on GitHub's cloud servers even when your local Mac is shut down.
